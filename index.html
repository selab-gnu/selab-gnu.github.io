SEA Lab, Since Spring 2016

We aim to solve the software evolution problem by utilizing software architecture, if possible. Our lab consists of one professor, three Ph.D students, two master students and three interns. It is located in Unit 804, Block 601, Gyeongsang National University, Jinju, Repulic of Korea. This homepage is under the construction.

Lab Principles

SEA Lab is managed under the following principles:

1. Self-determination: Lab members operate the lab office by themselves. Lab members and visitors can pursue the research topics that they want to do in the research area of software engineering. 

2. Openness: Lab members are open to the collaboration with other researchers and other labs. Visitors can freely participate in lab activities, while they are staying in the lab.  

3. Ethics: Lab members and visitors should comply with Software Engineering Code of Ethics and Professional Practice (cf. [English] and [Korean])
